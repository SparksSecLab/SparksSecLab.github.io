// LEARNING APPROACH

Document the Process

Each scenario is documented around the problem, available information, troubleshooting decisions, testing, remediation, verification, and final findings.

The goal is not simply to make the problem disappear. The goal is to understand why the problem occurred, make controlled changes, verify the result, and document what was learned.
